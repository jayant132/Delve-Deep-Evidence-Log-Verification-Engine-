# Postmortem: INC-0058

**Service:** order-service
**Date:** 2026-06-20
**Severity:** Low

## Summary
Order confirmation emails were delayed by up to 20 minutes due to a
backlog in the notification queue after a spike in order volume during
a flash sale.

## Timeline
- 12:00 - Flash sale traffic begins
- 12:15 - Notification queue depth exceeds normal baseline by 8x
- 12:40 - Queue backlog clears naturally as traffic subsides

## Root Cause
Notification worker pool was not scaled for promotional traffic spikes.

## Resolution
No immediate action needed; queue self-recovered. Added autoscaling
rule for notification workers tied to queue depth.

## Lessons Learned
Notification infrastructure should scale independently ahead of known
high-traffic events (sales, promotions).
