# Postmortem: INC-0051

**Service:** auth-service
**Date:** 2026-06-02
**Severity:** Medium

## Summary
Login failures increased to 5% due to an expired TLS certificate on the
identity provider integration. Not related to any deployment.

## Timeline
- 03:00 - Certificate expired
- 03:12 - First TLS handshake failures logged
- 08:45 - On-call engineer noticed elevated error rate
- 09:10 - Certificate renewed and rotated

## Root Cause
Manual certificate renewal process was missed; no automated expiry alert
existed at the time.

## Resolution
Emergency certificate renewal. Added 30-day-expiry monitoring alert.

## Lessons Learned
All externally-facing TLS certificates need automated expiry monitoring,
not manual tracking.
